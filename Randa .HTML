Destination Guide

The Island of Serendipity

Ancient cities, sacred temples, tea-carpeted mountains, the world's best leopard safaris and endless golden coast — all on one small island.

Culture & Heritage

Sigiriya & the Ancient Cities

Climb the legendary Lion Rock, wander Anuradhapura's stupas and admire Dambulla's golden cave temples.

Mountains & Tea Country

Nuwara Eliya & Ella

Ride the scenic blue train through tea estates, past waterfalls and over the Nine Arch Bridge.

Culture & Heritage

Sacred Kandy

The Temple of the Tooth, the Esala Perahera and the island's last royal capital.

Wildlife & Nature

Yala & the National Parks

The densest leopard population on Earth, plus elephants, sloth bears and painted lagoons.

Food & Local Experiences

Living Traditions

Stilt fishermen, spice gardens, tea plucking and home-cooked Sri Lankan feasts.

Adventure

Trails, Rapids & Peaks

Trek Adam's Peak, raft the Kelani River and mountain-bike through cloud forest.

Beaches & Coast

Galle Fort & the Southern Coast

A UNESCO-listed Dutch fort, boutique cafes, stilt fishermen and sunset rampart walks — with Mirissa and Bentota just down the coast.

Beaches & Coast

Trincomalee & the East

powder-soft Nilaveli sands, Pigeon Island snorkeling and the deep-blue harbor of the east.

Culture & Heritage

Colombo

Galle Face Green sunsets, buzzing markets and a food scene blending every culture of the island.
